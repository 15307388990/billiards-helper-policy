About this policy

This policy applies to Billiard Aim Practice, published on Google Play by Created, package com.ming.billiard. Some app interfaces use the names “Billiard Aim” or “台瞄”; these refer to the same app. Contact the developer at lan.sha@163.com for privacy questions or data requests.

1. Screen content and selected images stay on your device

The live assistance feature uses Android MediaProjection only after you choose to start it and grant the system permission. It can read what is visible on the captured display, including other apps. Frames are processed in memory on your device to recognize the table and draw visual reference lines; we do not upload these frames or selected practice images to our membership server, Firebase or Sentry. Sentry screenshot attachment is disabled. Calibration, settings, and benchmark results may be stored locally. You can stop capture using Android’s capture controls or the app’s floating controls, and revoke overlay permission in Android settings. The app does not record microphone audio or use the camera for this feature.

Created operates the membership and support service, including the API at remote-google-api.chiyoukeji.cn. Hosting providers process service records on our behalf. Google Play processes purchases and related verification. Firebase/Google services provide analytics, app-instance and related service functions; Sentry provides error and diagnostic reporting. Data is transmitted to these providers as needed for the purposes above, under their applicable service and privacy terms. We do not sell your personal data or use the app to display personalized advertisements.

4. Notice, permissions and your choices

Updated builds present this policy and an explanation of online membership, usage analytics and diagnostics before enabling those services. Screen capture and overlay access require separate user actions and Android permissions. Declining the initial notice closes the activation screen without starting those online services. Updated builds disable advertising-ID collection and advertising personalization signals. You can stop using the app, revoke Android permissions, clear local app data or uninstall it. Clearing/uninstalling does not itself delete records already held on servers or cancel a Google Play subscription.

5. Security and retention

Online service traffic uses HTTPS/TLS. Access to service records is restricted to authorized support/administrative use and service providers. No system can promise absolute security. We keep membership, transaction and operational records while needed to provide access, verify purchases, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and meet applicable obligations. Not every record currently has an automatic expiry. When data is no longer needed or a verified deletion request is fulfilled, we delete or de-identify applicable records; necessary purchase/fraud/legal records may be retained. Backups and provider-held records can remain subject to their retention settings. We will explain applicable retention limitations when responding to a request.

6. Access, correction and deletion requests

You can request access, correction or deletion by emailing lan.sha@163.com with the subject Billiard Aim Practice data deletion request. A request is available even if you no longer have the app. Include the app name and, if available, a support identifier (if one has been provided to you) or Google Play order number so we can locate the relevant records. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify the request. See the data-deletion request page.

We review requests for device/membership records, interaction history and associated diagnostics and arrange deletion or de-identification where applicable. Some transaction, fraud-prevention, legal or backup records may need to remain as explained above. Removing app data does not cancel a subscription: manage or cancel it in Google Play subscriptions. Using the service again can create new service records.

7. Children and changes

The app is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has supplied personal data, contact us so we can review and address it. We will publish updates to this policy on this page with a revised date and provide an updated in-app notice when needed.
