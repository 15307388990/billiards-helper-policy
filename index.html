Privacy Policy

This application ("the App") respects and protects user privacy.

1. Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.

2. Screen Content Processing

The App may request screen capture permission (MediaProjection) for visual assistance purposes. All screen data is processed locally on the user's device and is not stored or transmitted to any server.

3. Device Information

The App does not collect unique device identifiers.

4. Third-Party Services

The App may use Google Play services for distribution and payment processing. Any data handling by Google complies with their privacy policy.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13.

6. Updates

This policy may be updated in accordance with applicable laws.

If you have any questions, please contact us via the contact information provided in the App.
