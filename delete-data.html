How to request deletion

  1. Email lan.sha@163.com with the subject “Billiard Aim Practice data deletion request”.
  2. Identify the app and include your support identifier or Google Play order number, if available. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.
  3. We may verify the request before deleting or de-identifying applicable device/membership records, interaction history and associated diagnostic records. We will explain any records we must retain, such as necessary purchase, fraud-prevention, legal or backup records.

No login or installed app is required to make a request. Uninstalling alone does not remove server records. To stop subscription charges, also cancel through Google Play. Using the app again may create new records.
